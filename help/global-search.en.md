# How global search works

The search bar at the top center of every screen lets you find **tasks** and
**messages** without leaving where you are. Type your query and press Enter
(or the magnifying-glass button).

## What you can search for

- **By keyword.** Type a word or phrase and search checks the title/subject,
  status, priority, and body content (task description, message text).
- **In natural language, when available.** If your organization has AI
  interpretation configured, you can also ask questions like "urgent tasks
  this week" or "pending messages from Antonio" and search understands
  relative dates, statuses, and priorities. If AI isn't configured or doesn't
  respond in time, search still works by keyword — you never lose results
  because of that.

## Examples

| You type | It finds |
|---|---|
| `zeta contract` | Tasks or messages whose title/subject or content mention "zeta contract" |
| `urgent tasks this week` | Tasks with urgent priority and a due date within this week (requires AI) |
| `pending messages from Antonio` | Messages from Antonio with pending status (requires AI) |
| `budget meeting` | Any task or message mentioning both words, in any field |

## If search doesn't understand your question

Sometimes search needs more information before it can answer (for example,
if you ask for "yesterday's task" and there are several). In that case it
will ask a follow-up question instead of results. Just type your answer in
the same bar and press Enter — no need to repeat the original question,
search remembers the context.

## What you can't search yet

- Notes and attachment filenames aren't included in search yet.
- Only the models your organization has enabled for global search are
  covered — today that's tasks and messages.
- Results respect your permissions: you'll never see something you
  couldn't see by opening the record directly.

## Tip

If you're looking for something very specific (an identifier, a reference
number, an exact word), a short keyword is usually faster and more precise
than a long question.
